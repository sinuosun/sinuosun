## About Me

I am a B.Eng. candidate in Robotics Engineering at Peking University. I am interested in how aquatic animals sense their surroundings and how those principles can inform sensing systems for underwater robots. My research experience spans artificial lateral-line sensing and underwater electrosensing.

## Research Interests

- **Bio-inspired robotics and underwater sensing:** artificial lateral lines, electrosensing, and robot localization
- **Embedded systems:** sensor integration and multichannel data acquisition
- **Machine learning:** statistical learning and experimental evaluation

## Research Experience

### Artificial Lateral-Line Sensing for Biomimetic Robotic Fish

*Independent undergraduate research project · Peking University · Sep 2026–present*

I am developing a pressure-based artificial lateral-line prototype, initially focused on estimating depth and attitude in still water. I ran an on-device benchmark using synthetic pressure data to assess the processing pipeline. Physical sensor acquisition and experimental validation are pending.

### Underwater Electrosensing and Robot Localization

*Undergraduate research participant · Peking University · Dec 2025–Aug 2026*

I supported the integration and debugging of an underwater electrosensing test platform. I assisted with experimental parameter tuning and data acquisition scripts, ran repeated trials, and organized sensor data for validation.
